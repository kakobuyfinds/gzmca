Ordering and shipping with JoyaGoo

Check the listing before you order

  1. Open the CAFANS product page, then choose JoyaGoo under Buy Link when available. Compare the seller, item and selected variant on the destination page.
  2. Compare the seller’s size chart with a similar item you own. For shoes, check which foot or insole measurements the seller provides. For tops, record chest width and garment length.
  3. If warehouse QC photos are available, compare the selected color, labels and measurements with your order before approving shipment. For patterned items, check placement in both close-up and full-item photos.
  4. Get quotes for the same destination, packed weight and parcel dimensions. Check the quoted billable weight and which fees are included before comparing totals.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the spreadsheet links go?

The main buttons open CAFANS. Product links open their individual CAFANS listings, where you can inspect details and choose an available shopping agent.

Are shipping and taxes included in the displayed prices?

The catalog shows estimated item prices in USD. Add domestic delivery, international shipping, service fees and applicable taxes using quotes for your actual parcel and destination.

Where can I see QC photos for my order?

The images here come from the CAFANS product listings. If your agent provides warehouse QC photos, look for them in your order or warehouse account and match them to the variant you selected.
